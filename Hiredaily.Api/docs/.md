# 1. User Registration and Login
- User registers as a job seeker with their basic details.
- User logs in with their registered credentials.

# 2. Company Registration and Login
- Companies register themselves by providing company details.
- The company admin logs in with their registered credentials.

# 3. Job Posting & Matching
- Employer posts a job.
- System filters workers based on:
  - Skills
  - Availability
- Workers see matching jobs.

# 4. Application & Selection
- Workers apply for the job.
- Employer selects one worker.

# 5. Job Acceptance
- Selected worker is notified.
- Worker accepts the job within a time window.

# 6. Wage Locking
- Wage is reserved/locked once the job is accepted.

# 7. Job Execution
- Worker travels to the job site.
- Job is performed.

# 8. Completion & Payment
- Both employer and worker confirm completion.
- Wage is released to the worker.

# 9. Dispute Resolution
- Either party raises a dispute.
- Escalation team resolves the conflict.
